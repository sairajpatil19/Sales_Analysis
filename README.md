# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior & Sales Analysis

An end-to-end **Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis** project using **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to uncover customer purchasing patterns, sales trends, product performance, and business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer shopping data to understand how different customer characteristics and purchasing behaviors influence revenue and business performance.

The analysis combines:

- 🐘 PostgreSQL for business-oriented SQL analysis
- 🐍 Python for data exploration and analysis
- 📊 Power BI for interactive visualization and reporting

The goal is to transform raw customer transaction data into actionable insights that can support better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The project focuses on answering important business questions such as:

- How does revenue differ between male and female customers?
- Do customers who use discounts still make high-value purchases?
- Which products have the highest average ratings?
- Do Express shipping customers spend more than Standard shipping customers?
- Do subscribed customers generate more revenue?
- Which products have the highest discount rates?
- How can customers be segmented based on previous purchases?
- What are the most purchased products within each category?
- Are repeat buyers more likely to subscribe?
- Which age groups contribute the most revenue?

---

# 🔍 SQL Analysis

The SQL analysis was performed using **PostgreSQL**.

### 1. Revenue by Gender

Compared total revenue generated by male and female customers.

### 2. Discount Usage & High-Value Purchases

Identified customers who used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount.

### 3. Top-Rated Products

Identified the top 5 products based on average customer review ratings.

### 4. Shipping Analysis

Compared average purchase amounts between:

- Standard Shipping
- Express Shipping

### 5. Subscription Analysis

Compared subscribers and non-subscribers based on:

- Number of customers
- Average spending
- Total revenue

### 6. Discount Rate by Product

Identified products with the highest percentage of purchases where discounts were applied.

### 7. Customer Segmentation

Customers were categorized into:

- **New**
- **Returning**
- **Loyal**

based on their previous purchase history.

### 8. Top Products by Category

Used SQL window functions to identify the **top 3 most purchased products within each category**.

### 9. Repeat Buyers & Subscription

Analyzed whether customers with more than 5 previous purchases were more likely to subscribe.

### 10. Revenue by Age Group

Calculated the total revenue contributed by each customer age group.
